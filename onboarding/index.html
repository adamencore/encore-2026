    What we do and do not collect. This form stores your contact details, emergency contact, and any allergy, dietary or accessibility needs you tell us, in a private Encore spreadsheet that only staff can open. We use it to keep you safe and fed at rehearsal. We never collect your Social Security number, taxpayer ID or bank details on this website. Those go into QuickBooks only, through the invite you receive by email. If you type something that looks like one into a box here, the system will refuse to save it and ask you to put it in QuickBooks instead.
