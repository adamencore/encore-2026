Fall term 2026

Six classes, one showcase

Most classes run September through December and finish together on the Electric Theater stage. The Audition Workshop runs as three standalone sessions.

Grow With a Pro Mondays · 4:00–5:00pm

Finding Your Voice in Tap

An intensive tap workshop with Tuacahn actor Anthony Savino, for intermediate to advanced dancers. Ages 12–18Sept 7 to Oct 19 · seven weeks with Anthony Savino Workshop Second Monday · 4:30–6:00pm

Audition Workshop

Walk into your next audition with a monologue and a song you trust. Ages 12–18Sept 14, Oct 12 & Nov 9 with Randy Sly Class Tuesdays · 4:00–5:00pm

Musical Theatre Foundations

Step into the spotlight and discover the magic of Broadway. Ages 7+Sept 8 to Dec 9 with Haven Record Class Tuesdays · 5:00–6:00pm

Musical Theatre Jazz

Jazz technique for the stage, featuring combinations from A Chorus Line. Ages 10+Sept 8 to Dec 9 with Mattea Denney Class Wednesdays · 4:00–5:30pm

Freestyle Hip Hop

Grooves, rhythm, and the freedom of the dance circle. Ages 7+Sept 9 to Dec 9 with Clayton “C-Bass” Ashcraft Class Saturdays · 2:00–3:00pm

The Art of Improv

Build a story with no script, and trust the people beside you. Ages 7+Sept 12 to Dec 9 with Jacob Merrill
