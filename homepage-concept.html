Our home

Performing at the historic Electric Theater

Since 2021, Encore has called one of southern Utah's most storied venues home, a downtown St. George landmark built in 1911 with three-foot-thick adobe walls. It was the first motion picture theater in southern Utah, and the first air-conditioned building in St. George.

Our mission is to inspire discovery and imagination through exploration and play, on a century-old stage in the middle of downtown.

Here's how to get started

1

Choose a program

Camps and Junior classes start at age 7. Emerging Artists take bigger roles, Signature Series is the full mainstage, and Aspire is our audition-based touring company.

2

Audition or register

Classes and camps are open registration. Shows hold a vocal audition and a dance call, with callbacks where a production needs them.

3

Take the stage

Rehearse with working theater professionals, then perform at the Historic Electric Theater in front of your people.
