Aspire Performing Co.

An all-ages show choir for song, dance and the stage.

Aspire is Encore Performing Arts' audition-based show choir in St. George, Utah. Young Artists train together every week, build real stage skills, and share the spotlight in a full production each semester.

6+

Ages 6 through senior year

Open to Young Artists from age 6 through high school seniors. High school graduates are not eligible.

Wkly

Rehearsals every week

Weekly rehearsals each semester, right on stage at the historic Electric Theater.

2x

Auditions twice a year

Auditions open each semester, with a waitlist between rounds.

$395

About per semester

Payment plans and limited scholarships are available, so cost is never the reason to sit out.
