PNG + HEIC to WebP

Drop in PNGs or iPhone photos (HEIC) and get back smaller WebP files for the website. Everything happens on your device, so images never leave your computer.

  • PNG + HEIC
  • No uploads
  • Batch + ZIP
  • Works offline
80 to 85 is the sweet spot for most web images. 100 is near lossless.
Resizes larger images down. 1600 is the site standard for full-width photos. iPhone photos are about 4000 wide, so set this for those.
No files yet.

    Good to know

    • Works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Older Safari cannot make WebP files and will show a message.
    • HEIC is the iPhone photo format. The first HEIC you add loads a one-time photo reader (about 800 KB), then it is instant.
    • Converted files drop camera and location data automatically, including GPS on iPhone photos.
    • When replacing an image on the site, give the new file a new name so browsers pick it up.
