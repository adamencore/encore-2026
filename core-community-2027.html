Become part of The Core

The Core is Encore’s community of supporters who give small, regular monthly donations: the steady support that keeps our programs strong and growing.

Why monthly matters

Small gifts, a world of difference.

Monthly gifts of any size add up to real impact for hundreds of young performers, funding arts education, scholarships, and a place to shine on stage.

Steady support lets us plan the season ahead with confidence, from the first rehearsal to the final bow.

Where it goes

What your gift makes possible

Every monthly gift goes to work in three places.

Arts education

Scholarships

Member thank-yous

What members receive

As a thank-you for your ongoing support, members of The Core enjoy:

  • Opening night receptions

    Join us at opening night receptions, and bring a guest.

  • Production Prologue

    An invitation to Encore’s Production Prologue: a cast meet and greet before the show.

  • Impact newsletter

    Our monthly newsletter with updates on the difference your gift is making.

  • Listed in playbills

    Your name listed in our program playbills as part of The Core.

  • Tax receipt

    A charitable contribution tax receipt. Encore is a registered 501(c)(3).

  • Lasting impact

    You become an ongoing, integral part of Encore’s success and growth.

How to join

Three steps, about a minute

  1. 01

    Click Join The Core

    The button opens Encore’s secure Givebutter donation page in a new tab.

  2. 02

    Choose Monthly Payments

    In the frequency dropdown, select Monthly Payments.

  3. 03

    Pick your amount

    Any amount counts. $25 a month is about the price of a night at the movies.
