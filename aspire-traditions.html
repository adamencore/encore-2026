A new program

Introducing Aspire: Traditions

Aspire: Traditions is a brand-new performance program created to celebrate one of our favorite experiences: performing at the Disneyland Resort.

Over the years, we’ve loved taking our Young Artists to Disneyland, and now we’re making it a tradition. Aspire: Traditions is a special travel performance program that gives talented Young Artists the opportunity to create lifelong memories while representing Aspire Performing Company on one of the most magical stages in the world.

This March, our first-ever Aspire: Traditions group will travel to Disney California Adventure Park for a professional performance opportunity, and we can’t wait to share this experience with the Young Artists who join us.

Ages 12–18

Who should audition

We’re seeking energetic, dedicated, and experienced Young Artists ages 12–18 who are ready to take on more demanding music and choreography, travel together as a group, and represent Aspire Performing Company at a professional performance venue.
