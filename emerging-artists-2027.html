About Emerging Artists

Where Young Artists grow into real performers.

Building on Camps and Junior Shows, this tier casts Young Artists ages 12 to 14 in full licensed productions with bigger roles, sharper choreography, and deeper training. It is the step where technique, stage presence, and responsibility all start to come together.

FAQ

Emerging Artist questions

Don’t see your question? Reach out, we’re glad to help.

Who are Emerging Artist Shows for?
This tier is built for Young Artists ages 12 to 14 who are ready to grow, taking on bigger roles, deeper training, and full productions.
How is this different from Junior Shows?
Junior Shows are a Young Artist’s first full productions, with the basics taught well. Emerging Artists raises the bar: more demanding material, sharper choreography, featured and lead roles, and more responsibility on and off stage.
Are these full productions?
Yes. Emerging Artists are cast in complete, fully staged licensed musicals with a real rehearsal process and a real run, not a short-form showcase.
Does my Young Artist need experience?
Some stage experience helps, but it isn’t required. Many Emerging Artists come up through Camps and Junior Shows; others join here. Reach out and we’ll help you find the right starting point.
How do auditions work?
Productions are cast by audition each season. See what’s currently open on our auditions page, where dates and how to register are posted for each show.
What comes after Emerging Artists?
The next step is our Signature Shows, Encore’s most advanced, artist-driven productions for ages 13 to 18.

Ready to step up?

Emerging Artists is where Young Artists grow into real performers. We’re happy to help you find the right next production.
