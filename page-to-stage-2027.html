More than a night at the theater

Page to Stage pairs educational workshops with group trips to professional productions, exploring musicals and plays through the lens of storytelling, history, culture, and performance. A front-row education.

Each trip is a single event with limited seating. Reserve a spot, and we’ll send a secure link to pay and confirm: simple, with no hoops to jump through.

New format

Page to Stage Pro Shot Edition

We love taking families on the road to see live theater, and we also bring Broadway home. In Pro Shot Edition, we show a licensed, professionally filmed Broadway performance on the Electric Theater’s big screen, paired with the same Page to Stage workshop on the story, characters, and themes behind the show. It’s the same learning, with no road trip required.

How registration works

Reserve, pay, you’re in

A simple process, the same way we handle auditions. No online cart or checkout.

  1. 1

    Reserve your spot

    Fill out the quick registration form for the trip. It takes a couple of minutes and holds your interest while seats remain.

  2. 2

    Get a secure payment link

    Encore emails you a secure link to pay. Payment is handled by a trusted processor; your card details never touch our website.

  3. 3

    You’re confirmed

    Once payment is complete, your spot is locked in. We’ll follow up with trip details, timing, and anything to bring.

Questions before you register? Call (435) 414-0049 or email hello@encorepa.org.

FAQ

Questions about Page to Stage

Don’t see your question? Call (435) 414-0049 or email hello@encorepa.org.

What is Page to Stage?
Page to Stage is a field-trip program that pairs educational workshops with group trips to professional productions. Open to students and community members, it explores popular musicals and plays through the lens of storytelling, history, culture, and performance, moving beyond surface-level entertainment to the creative process and purpose behind the art.
How do I register and pay?
Reserve your spot through the registration form. Because seating is limited, payment is handled separately: after you register, we email you a secure payment link to confirm your place. There’s no checkout on the website itself.
Who can take part?
Page to Stage is open to students and community members. Specific age ranges and any prerequisites are listed on each event. Questions? Call (435) 414-0049.
Is seating limited?
Yes, these events have limited seating and tend to fill quickly, so register early. Your spot is confirmed once payment is complete.
What’s included in the fee?
The fee covers the live show experience and Encore’s accompanying workshop. What each event includes is listed on the event itself.
What is Pro Shot Edition?
A licensed, professionally filmed Broadway performance shown on the Electric Theater’s big screen, paired with the same Page to Stage workshop. Same learning, no road trip required.
