Programs

Find the right stage for your Young Artist

From a first playful class to a full mainstage role, every Encore program is taught by working theater professionals. Here is a closer look at each path, and where it leads.

Six paths

A place for every performer

From a first playful class to a full mainstage role, here is the full picture of each path.

01

Seasonal

Musical Theater Camps

Ages 5–12

Week-long intensives where Young Artists rehearse a show fast and perform it live by the end of the week. Days of acting, voice, and choreography build toward a real production, the quickest way to fall in love with the stage.

  • One week
  • Ends in a performance
  • No experience needed
Explore camps
02

First stages

Junior

Ages 7–13

Playful first classes and shows where big imaginations take their very first bow. Young Artists learn the fundamentals, stage presence, teamwork, and storytelling, through games, songs, and scenes built for brand-new performers.

  • Beginner-friendly
  • No experience needed
  • Full productions
Explore Junior
03

Building skills

Emerging Artists

Ages 12–14

Real technique and real scripts for performers ready to step into bigger roles. Training deepens across acting, vocal work, and movement as Young Artists take on featured parts in full productions.

  • Intermediate
  • Featured roles
  • Acting, voice & movement
Explore Emerging Artists
04

Mainstage

Signature Shows

Ages 13–18

Full mainstage productions for our most experienced Young Artists. The Signature Series stages ambitious titles with full sets, live music, and the polish that prepares artists for whatever comes next.

  • Audition-based
  • Live music
  • Full production values
Explore Signature Shows
05

Show choir

Aspire Performing Co.

Ages 5–18

Encore’s audition-based show choir. Aspire blends song and dance into high-energy showcases, performing across southern Utah throughout the year.

  • Audition-based
  • Song & dance
  • Showcases all year
Explore Aspire
06

Field trips

Page to Stage

Ages 8–18

Theater field trips that turn a night at the show into a lesson. Young Artists and families attend professional productions together, then dig into how the magic is made.

  • Field trips
  • See theater up close
  • Families welcome
Explore Page to Stage
