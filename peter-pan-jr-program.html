Before the curtain

Welcome to Neverland

Thank you for being here. Every Young Artist on this stage has spent weeks rehearsing for tonight, and an audience is the last thing they need to make it fly.

  • Silence your phoneKeep this program open if you like. Just switch to silent.
  • Dim your screenTurn the brightness down during the show so the stage stays the brightest thing in the room.
  • No photos or video during the showRecording is not permitted under our performance license. Snap away at curtain call and in the lobby.
  • Cheer them onLaugh, clap and cheer. It is the best gift you can give a young performer.

Why we do this

Encore’s mission

Encore Performing Arts inspires and empowers young artists to discover and develop important life skills through unparalleled arts education, mentorship and extraordinary theater experiences.

Through the lens of acting, storytelling and theater-making, Young Artists develop skills in:

  • Collaboration and teamwork
  • Critical thinking and complex problem-solving
  • Giving and receiving constructive criticism
  • Communication
  • Confidence
  • Leadership
  • Empathy
