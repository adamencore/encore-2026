Concessions training

Your Responsibilities

Step 1

Before Doors Open

  • Arrive on time and check in with the box office manager.
  • Learn the house layout and seating before doors open.

Step 2

Tickets & Playbill

  • Scan or check tickets at the door.
  • Direct patrons to the digital playbill options (QR code or website) and share how to access it.
  • Limit printed programs to 1–2 per group.

Step 3

During the Show

  • Guide guests to their seats and keep aisles and exits clear.
  • Help latecomers find their seats at an appropriate moment.
  • Remind guests to silence phones. No flash photography or recording.
  • Watch for issues and report them to the box office manager.

Step 4

After the Show

  • Help tidy the house after the performance.

Step 1

Set Up

  • Set up the concessions table before doors open.
  • Make sure snacks, drinks, and Candygrams are stocked, priced, and ready to sell.

Step 2

During Sales

  • Accept payments as directed by the box office (cash, Venmo, or card).
  • Sell snacks and drinks before the show and at intermission.

Step 3

Candygrams Guide

  • Sell Candygrams (pre-packaged treats with a note).
  • Have guests fill out the recipient’s name and message.
  • Collect payment and place the completed Candygram in the designated delivery bin.

Step 4

Keep It Clean

  • Keep the area clean, organized, and stocked throughout the show.
  • Restock as needed (communicate with the box office if supplies run low).

Accessibility & Accommodations

  • Be attentive to all guests, including those with special needs.
  • Help with wheelchair seating and any accommodation requests.
  • When in doubt, ask the box office manager.
